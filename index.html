About the speaker

Reddi Kotha is Professor of Strategy and Entrepreneurship at Singapore Management University’s Lee Kong Chian School of Business, where he also serves as Academic Director of the PhD Program and Innovation & Entrepreneurship Research Peak Co-Coordinator. His research lies at the intersection of innovation, entrepreneurship, and organizational design, with publications in the Academy of Management Journal, Strategic Management Journal, Research Policy, Journal of Business Venturing, and Harvard Business Review. His awards include the Glueck Distinguished Paper Award and the Karen Legge Award from the Journal of Management Studies, and he has secured more than $900,000 in competitive research funding. He serves as an Associate Editor at Strategic Management Journal and previously served as an Associate Editor at the Academy of Management Journal from 2022 to 2025.

Assistant Professor of Strategy and Entrepreneurship · London Business School
