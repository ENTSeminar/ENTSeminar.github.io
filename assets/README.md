# Image sources

The header uses `iimb-logo.svg`, the IIM Bangalore wordmark displayed online. The primary IIMB
website blocked direct automated retrieval of its raster asset; the equivalent vector artwork was
retrieved from [Wikipedia's IIMB file page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:IIMB-wordmarklogo.svg).
Confirm institutional brand-use approval before public launch.

Speaker portraits were retrieved from the speakers' official institutional profiles on 3 September 2026.

- `reddi-kotha.jpg`: [Singapore Management University faculty profile](https://faculty.smu.edu.sg/profile/reddi-kotha-1961)
- `sankalp-pratap.png`: [Desai Sethi School of Entrepreneurship, IIT Bombay](https://www.dsse.iitb.ac.in/people/sankalp-pratap)
- `sarath-balachandran.jpg`: [London Business School faculty profile](https://www.london.edu/faculty-and-research/faculty-profiles/s/sarath-balachandran)
- `siddharth-vedula.jpg`: [Miami University Farmer School of Business directory](https://miamioh.edu/fsb/directory/?up=%2Fdirectory%2Fvedulas)
- `deepti-mishra.png`: [IIMB Digest profile](https://digest.iimb.ac.in/2026/01/dr-deepti-mishra/)

The five `*-enhanced.jpg` portraits are identity-preserving, web-optimized restorations made from
the institutional source images. Each is exported at 880 × 1100 pixels to stay crisp on high-density
displays without transferring an unnecessarily large master file. The originals remain alongside
them for provenance and easy replacement with speaker-approved photographs.
